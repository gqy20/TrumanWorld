# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Truman World serves AI researchers, creative practitioners, and product explorers who study
long-running multi-agent behavior. They use the director console to observe a living simulation,
trace events and relationships, inspect individual agents, and introduce conditions without
directly controlling a resident's thoughts.

## Product Purpose

Truman World makes a persistent AI society observable, explainable, and responsibly steerable.
Success means a user can understand what is happening in the town, why it happened, how the world
is changing, and when an intervention is warranted, while each resident remains an autonomous
subject rather than a scripted prop.

## Brand Personality

Observant, restrained, humane. The interface should feel like a quiet director's room overlooking
a living world: precise enough for research, legible enough for product work, and emotionally
aware without becoming theatrical or sentimental.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS dashboard where metric cards dominate and the simulated world becomes secondary.
- A game HUD or Minecraft clone that implies direct control, competition, or resource gameplay.
- A dense blue command console, decorative glass panels, nested cards, or permanent labels covering
  the world stage.
- A god-simulation framing that treats agents as puppets and intervention as unrestricted control.

## Design Principles

1. World first. The living stage is the primary source of truth; controls and metrics support it.
2. Show causality. Connect state, events, relationships, and interventions so changes can be traced.
3. Preserve agency. Use language and affordances that distinguish creating conditions from
   controlling thoughts.
4. Reveal complexity progressively. Keep observation calm by default and expose forensic detail on
   selection or request.
5. Make time visible. Current state, recent change, and historical replay must remain clearly
   distinguishable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA contrast as the implementation baseline. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus,
semantic labels, and readable status text without relying on color alone. Respect reduced-motion
preferences for camera movement, pulses, and event animation. Keep the SVG director map available
as a textual and low-GPU alternative to the 3D stage.
